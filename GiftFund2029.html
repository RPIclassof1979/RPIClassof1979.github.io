A place to connect

A familiar place, new possibilities

The Rensselaer Union has long been a place where friendships form and memories are made. Our gift focuses on enhancing the Rathskeller Events Center with technology that can help today’s students make the most of this shared space.

The Rathskeller Events Center is part of the broader Union project. The Class of 1979 is raising funds for digital displays, audiovisual systems, and future enhancements of this recently renovated area.

Fifty years after our graduation, we can help give new RPI generations a place to learn, celebrate, and connect. The Rathskeller Event Center will be used by students, clubs, fraternities, sororities, and alumni, keeping the Rensselaer Union as the vibrant center of student life at RPI.

What our gift funds: Technology and future enhancements for the Rathskeller Events Center. What it does not fund: Construction of the entire facility.
The purpose of our gift

Equip. Enrich. Sustain.

Three ways our Class Gift Fund is intended to make a difference. Specific purchases and future uses remain subject to RPI and Gift Committee confirmation.

01 / EQUIP

Equip the space

Help provide digital displays, audiovisual systems, and supporting technology for the Rathskeller Events Center.

02 / ENRICH

Enrich student experiences

Support a versatile setting for student events, presentations, performances, and gatherings through improved technology.

03 / SUSTAIN

Create a lasting legacy

Help establish a fund that may support future technology enhancements and student programming beyond our 50th Reunion.

Looking ahead

Our 50th Reunion is a Milestone, not the endpoint

The initial technology goal is only the beginning of the longer-term vision.

Our hope is to sustain a Class of 1979 fund that can help with future technology improvements and student programming after 2029. That is a meaningful way to connect our memories of the Rathskeller with the experiences of students yet to come.
